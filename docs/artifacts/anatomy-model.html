NEUROLOCALISER

The anatomy model — the substrate every localisation is derived from

A visual of structures.js + sites.js. Each structure maps to one examinable finding and sits at a level + part; a lesion site is one vascular territory on one side, and the structures sharing it fail together — that is how a named syndrome emerges, with no syndrome stored anywhere. Laterality is the crux: each finding is marked by the side it appears on relative to the lesion, set by whether its pathway has crossed at or below that level. In the cortex two further axes appear: a hemisphere gate (dominant vs non-dominant, resolved from the stated dominant side, default left) and vascular territory as a separate annotation — the anatomy is stated by subregion, and ACA/MCA/PCA stroke syndromes are composed from it, never baked in. This table is meant to be checked line-by-line by a neuroanatomist / neurologist.

Side of the deficit vs the lesion: IPSIsame side CONTRAopposite side (pathway has crossed) BILATboth sides (midline / commissural) MIDLINEno side (roots / sacral / sphincter) NONEnon-lateralised (higher-cortical / behavioural)

Derived compartments — nested syndromes by UNION

A compartment is not a primitive foramen — it is the union of the foramina it spans, gathered by composeSkullBaseSites(). The structure the union adds IS the discriminator, so no compartment is ever hand-listed.

cavernous_sinus = SOF ∪ rotundum
III · IV · VI · V1 · V2 · sympathetic (postganglionic Horner — no anhidrosis). V2 is exactly what separates it from the SOF.
orbital_apex = SOF ∪ optic_canal
SOF contents + optic neuropathy — monocular visual loss is the discriminator from the cavernous sinus.
collet_sicard = jugular ∪ hypoglossal
IX–XII together — the lower-cranial-nerve staircase beyond Vernet (the jugular-foramen primitive).
villaret = collet_sicard + carotid_space
IX–XII + sympathetic (Horner) — the most caudal / retropharyngeal step of the staircase.

How sites compose into syndromes

A site is derived, not hand-listed. Primitives are one part on one side; composites join parts. The named syndrome is a lookup on the emergent site — remove the phonebook and the engine still localises. In the cortex the primitive is a subregion and the vascular composites are just one lesion-shape family — the anatomy is pathology-agnostic.

No site holds both spasticity + wasting → ALS spans ≥2 sites (pathology layer). FND deferred.
