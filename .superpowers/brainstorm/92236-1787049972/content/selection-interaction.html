How should selecting a pathology work?

Same case in all three: L5 radiculopathy, with Spinal epidural abscess selected. Site urgency is routine; the abscess is a red must-not-miss, so the badge must flip to emergency. Watch where that flip is signalled.

A

Click the cause in What — Next swaps below it

Cause rows are clickable. The selected row gets the terracotta marker, and the Next card's lower two tiers re-render with a dismissible chip naming the pathology. Urgency flips inside the Next card.
